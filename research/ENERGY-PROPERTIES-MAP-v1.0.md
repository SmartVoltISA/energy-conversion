# ENERGY PROPERTIES MAP v1.0

**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH MAP — hypotheses, not a finished physical theory

## 1. Core distinction

We do not treat all energy-related quantities as the same kind of thing.

`STATE → DIFFERENCE → TRANSITION → BALANCE → NEW STATE`

Working interpretation:

- **Potential** — capacity for possible change.
- **Difference** — distinction between states/regions/conditions.
- **Memory** — difference that persists in the internal state.
- **Asymmetry** — a difference that makes forward and reverse evolution non-equivalent in the chosen description.
- **Work** — physical energy transfer associated with a directed change of state through an interaction.
- **Balance** — accounting relation between internal change and exchange with the environment.

## 3. Important proposed relationships

### Density

Density is not a new kind of energy. It is energy relative to a chosen measure of space or matter:

`energy density = energy / volume`

Analogous densities can be defined per mass or area.

### Gradient

A spatial difference can provide a direction for a physical flux when a coupling exists.

`difference + coupling → flux`

A gradient by itself is not guaranteed to produce useful work.

### Force

For a conservative mechanical coordinate, force can be related to the spatial gradient of potential energy:

`F = -∇U`

This is a particularly useful test case for the broader hypothesis that directed change can arise from a potential difference together with a coupling law.

### Power

Power makes the time component explicit:

`P = dW/dt`

Thus the distinction is useful:

`potential → available possibility`

`work → realized transfer/change`

`power → rate of realized transfer`

## 4. Memory as difference

Working hypothesis:

`M(t) = persistent part of ΔSTATE`

A system with the same visible structural state can behave differently if its hidden/internal state differs because of history.

Therefore the complete state may need to be:

`STATE = visible structure + internal memory`

This is compatible with hysteresis, stored strain, magnetization, polarization, chemical state, charge state, and other physical examples, but the exact mapping must be tested case by case.

## 5. Balance

The fundamental accounting test remains:

`E_in - E_out = ΔE_system + E_rejected`

A claimed conversion mechanism is not accepted merely because an output changes. The internal state change and rejected energy must be included in the account.

## 6. Candidate hierarchy

A useful working hierarchy is:

`POTENTIAL`
→ capacity for change

`DIFFERENCE`
→ distinction between states

`GRADIENT / FIELD`
→ spatial or configurational organization of difference

`COUPLING`
→ mechanism allowing the difference to act on another degree of freedom

`FORCE / FLUX`
→ directed transfer mechanism

`WORK`
→ energy transferred through the interaction

`POWER`
→ work per time

`MEMORY`
→ persistent internal difference

`BALANCE`
→ accounting closure

`DISSIPATION`
→ redistribution into less directly recoverable degrees of freedom

## 7. What must not be conflated

- Potential ≠ energy already transferred.
- Density ≠ force.
- Gradient ≠ energy by itself.
- Force ≠ energy.
- Power ≠ energy.
- Memory ≠ energy.
- Dissipation ≠ disappearance of energy.
- Model cost ≠ physical work.
- A mathematical asymmetry ≠ physical irreversibility.

## 8. Experimental consequence

The next physical tests should deliberately measure multiple properties at once:

1. initial and final state;
2. internal difference;
3. stored/memory state;
4. input energy;
5. output energy;
6. force/flux where applicable;
7. power as a function of time;
8. rejected energy;
9. repeatability and uncertainty;
10. whether the balance closes.

The goal is not to rename known quantities. The goal is to determine whether the proposed state/difference/memory/balance architecture predicts measurable behavior across different physical converters.

## 9. Epistemic status

This map is a research scaffold. It combines standard physical quantities with a proposed relational interpretation. The interpretation is provisional until tested against calibrated physical systems.

**UNKNOWN ≠ TRUE.**
**Calculation ≠ Experiment.**
**Correlation ≠ mechanism.**
