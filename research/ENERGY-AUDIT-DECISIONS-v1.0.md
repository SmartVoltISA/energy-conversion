# ENERGY AUDIT — DECISIONS v1.0

## Decisions made 2026-09-04

1. The legacy Ω-Lab branch is evidence/provenance, not established physical theory.
2. E-ENERGY-0014 is a new/native continuation; the legacy numbering gap 0014–0019 is not filled artificially.
3. E-ENERGY-0015 is a mechanism-isolation toy model. Its cost variable is not physical work.
4. E-ENERGY-0032 and 0032R remain separate; exact quantitative reproduction is unresolved.
5. E-ENERGY-0036 is provenance-sensitive and must be reconciled against the authoritative source before stronger claims are promoted.
6. E-ENERGY-0037 is a bifurcation result, not an energy-generation result; detailed primary-source data should be preserved before quantitative reuse.
7. EXP-0001 is the bridge to physical experiments and requires independent energy accounting.
8. The first physical test should favor low energy, controlled coupling, high measurement completeness and a closed boundary.
9. No claim of "free energy", energy creation, or a new physical definition of energy is permitted without a closed independent account.
10. Every graph edge in the research architecture requires an evidence status and provenance.

## Immediate priority

Build one reproducible physical converter experiment around:

`difference → coupling → measurable flow → useful work → rejected energy → balance`.

Candidate families: electrical RC/RL, magnetic actuator/loop, or mechanical spring/damper. Selection must be based on measurement completeness rather than novelty.
