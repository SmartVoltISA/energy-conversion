# E-ENERGY-0020 — Transition Barrier / Constrained Change

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Result

A structural state may have an accessible transition space while particular changes are blocked by explicit constraints. The experiment separates **available transformations** from the **barrier/path required to reach them**.

The result supports the distinction:

```text
STATE → ACCESSIBLE TRANSITIONS → BARRIER → TRANSITION
```

A barrier is not itself energy. It is a property of the chosen dynamics/constraints. The experiment therefore strengthens the need to model transition mechanism rather than infer energy from static topology alone.

## Boundary

Toy relational model only; no physical energy is established.
