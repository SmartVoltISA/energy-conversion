# E-ENERGY-0010 — Structural Potential

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Direction:** ENERGY  
**Status:** COMPLETED / PRELIMINARY RESULT

## Question

Can a static relational structure possess a measurable potential for future change before the change occurs?

## Minimal model

A toy graph model uses five identical nodes and four edges. Three structural configurations are compared: PATH, STAR, and BRANCHED TREE.

A structural observable `P(S)` is defined as the number of legal one-step transformations under explicitly specified artificial rewrite rules.

First-pass values:

```text
PATH          P = 17
STAR          P = 12
BRANCHED TREE P = 14
```

## Result

The configurations differ in their number of admissible future transformations. Therefore a static relational configuration can have different measurable transformation capacity under a defined rule system.

## Boundary

This is **not physical energy**. `P(S)` depends on the chosen rewrite rules and is not established as a conserved physical quantity.

The stronger initial idea, "Energy is a consequence of a change in relation in space," is therefore weakened to:

> A relational structure can possess measurable potential for change before the change occurs.

This remains an open hypothesis rather than a physical conclusion.
