# E-ENERGY-0028 — Conservative Transfer with Structural Barrier

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Result

The model combines an explicitly conserved transferable quantity with a structural transition barrier. Conservation and a barrier can coexist without producing an energetic release.

## Interpretation

This separates two ideas often conflated:

```text
CONSERVATION ≠ RELEASE
BARRIER ≠ ENERGY
```

A system may preserve a quantity while a particular transition remains difficult or inaccessible.

## Boundary

Toy model; no physical energy claim.
