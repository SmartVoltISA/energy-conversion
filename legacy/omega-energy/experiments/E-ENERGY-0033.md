# E-ENERGY-0033 — Spatial Channel with Green/Red/Blue States

**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Result

The channel-formation model was extended to a 31×31 spatial grid with three operational states represented as green/red/blue. Spatial structure reproduced qualitative channelization.

## Interpretation

The result shows that channel selection is not limited to an abstract graph: the same class of feedback can create spatially organized paths in the toy model.

## Boundary

The colors are labels for operational states. They are not evidence that color is a fundamental physical variable, and the model does not establish a physical energy mechanism.
