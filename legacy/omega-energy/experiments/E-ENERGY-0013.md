# E-ENERGY-0013 — Transition Asymmetry / No-Go Test

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** COMPLETED / NO-GO

## Result

The minimal relational model with symmetric/reversible transition rules does not generate intrinsic energetic directionality. Forward and reverse transitions remain structurally equivalent unless an additional asymmetry, state variable, history, reservoir, or non-reversible dynamics is introduced.

## Interpretation

This is a deliberate no-go result: structural potential and reversible transition rules are insufficient to derive physical energy directionality.

## Boundary

The result rejects the stronger claim for this model class, not the broader possibility of an energy-like quantity arising from richer dynamics.

## Consequence

Future experiments must distinguish:

```text
STRUCTURE → ACCESSIBLE CHANGE
TRANSITION → COST / RELEASE
CONSERVATION → TRANSFER
```

and must not encode the desired energetic asymmetry into the labels or rules.
