# E-ENERGY-0026 — Coupled Potential Transfer

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Result

Coupled subsystems were used to test whether a potential-like structural observable can be transferred through interaction. The model produced coupled redistribution while retaining an explicit accounting boundary.

## Interpretation

The useful abstraction becomes:

```text
STATE_A + STATE_B
      ↓ interaction
new STATE_A + new STATE_B
```

with candidate observables tracked on both sides. Transfer must be tested jointly with conservation rather than inferred from local change.

## Boundary

Toy relational experiment. It does not establish that the structural observable is physical energy.
