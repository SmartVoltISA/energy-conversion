# E-ENERGY-0022 — Emergent Storage from Relational History

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Result

The experiment tested whether history of prior relational transitions can leave a persistent state that changes future transition behavior. In the toy model, memory/history became a structural variable rather than an external annotation.

## Interpretation

A system's current state may need to include historical information to predict its future behavior. This supports the distinction between instantaneous structure and **state with memory**.

```text
STRUCTURE + HISTORY → EFFECTIVE STATE → FUTURE TRANSITIONS
```

## Boundary

The result is a toy-model observation and does not establish physical energy storage. It motivates testing whether history-dependent state can produce asymmetric transition costs while preserving explicit accounting.
