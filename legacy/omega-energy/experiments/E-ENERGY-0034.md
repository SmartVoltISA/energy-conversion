# E-ENERGY-0034 — Color as Structural Memory

**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Result

The experiment treated color/state labels as a possible visible carrier of structural memory in the spatial channel model.

## Interpretation

A visible state marker can encode history when the state itself affects subsequent dynamics. The important mechanism is state-dependent memory, not color as such.

## Boundary

Color is operational notation. The experiment does not establish that color is fundamental or that a specific color causes a physical chemical or energetic effect.
