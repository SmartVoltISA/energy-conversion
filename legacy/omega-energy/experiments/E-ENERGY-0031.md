# E-ENERGY-0031 — Channel Formation Under Limited Resource

**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Result

A limited resource, competing relations, positive feedback, and memory produced channel selection in the toy model.

## Interpretation

Under resource limitation, relational feedback can concentrate activity into selected paths rather than distribute it uniformly.

```text
LIMITED RESOURCE
      ↓
COMPETING RELATIONS
      ↓
FEEDBACK
      ↓
CHANNEL SELECTION
```

This is a structural/channelization result, not evidence for a new physical energy law.
