# E-ENERGY-0029 — Directionality

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Result

Directionality does not arise merely from ordering or naming of states. Reversible structural dynamics remain symmetric unless an additional physical/dynamical asymmetry is introduced.

## Interpretation

A potential difference can change sign under reversal, but that mathematical sign change is not by itself a physical rule for work, cost, or release.

## Boundary

Negative result for the tested relational model class. It motivates explicit testing of irreversibility, reservoirs, dissipation, and hidden degrees of freedom.
