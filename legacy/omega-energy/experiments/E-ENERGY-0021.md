# E-ENERGY-0021 — Transition Barrier Follow-up

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Result

The follow-up preserves the distinction between a state's transformation capacity and the difficulty of a particular transition. Structural constraints can create path dependence and blocked changes without requiring an energy variable.

## Boundary

This is a relational toy-model result. A transition barrier is not identified with physical energy or work.
