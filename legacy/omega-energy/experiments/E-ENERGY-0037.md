# E-ENERGY-0037 — Symmetric Bifurcation

**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Result

The final branch tested whether a symmetric dynamical setup can bifurcate into distinct states without an externally assigned directional label.

## Interpretation

Symmetry of the initial rules does not necessarily imply a single observed state; spontaneous state differentiation can arise through dynamics. However, this is not equivalent to energetic directionality: a bifurcation is a state-selection phenomenon, not a proof of energy generation or release.

## Boundary

Toy-model result. Any physical interpretation requires an explicit mapping to measurable variables and conservation accounting.
