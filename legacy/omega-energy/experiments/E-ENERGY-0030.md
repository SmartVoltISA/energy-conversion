# E-ENERGY-0030 — Local Release as Redistribution

**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Result

A toy conserved quantity `Q = A + B` was initialized at 10 and evolved through 50,000 trajectories. Local decreases in A could be accompanied by corresponding increases in B, preserving total Q.

## Interpretation

An apparent local release need not mean creation or destruction of the conserved quantity; it can be redistribution into another degree of freedom.

## Boundary

The model is not physical energy. The key methodological lesson is to close the accounting boundary before calling a local change a release.
