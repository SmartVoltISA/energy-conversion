# E-ENERGY-0036 — Emergence of Three States Without Color Labels

**Date:** 2026-08-23

## Result

The model was modified so that three states emerged without being prescribed as green/red/blue labels. This is stronger than simply assigning three colors in advance.

## Interpretation

When distinct states arise from the dynamics and are subsequently represented, the state distinction is a property of the model's dynamics rather than of the visualization labels.

## Boundary

This remains a toy relational/spatial result. It does not establish that nature fundamentally contains exactly three energetic states or that the states correspond to physical colors.
