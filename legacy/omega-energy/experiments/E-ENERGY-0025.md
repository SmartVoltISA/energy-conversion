# E-ENERGY-0025 — Matched Memory, Capacity and Different Transition Barriers

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Result

With matched memory/capacity conditions, different structural configurations can still exhibit different transition barriers. The result separates memory/capacity from the barrier of a particular transition.

## Interpretation

A system can have comparable stored/history state yet different accessibility of change because relational structure and transition constraints differ.

## Boundary

Toy-model result only; no identification with physical energy.
