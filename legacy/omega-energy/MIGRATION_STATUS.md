The exact original Ω-Lab repository remains the provenance source.

## Known research boundary

The legacy branch has not established a new physical definition of energy. The strongest surviving claims concern relational state, accessible transformations, barriers, memory, redistribution, channelization and the need for explicit physical accounting.

## Current continuation

The research is now deliberately moving from abstract relational models toward measurable conversion:

```text
potential → flow → converter → useful work → rejected energy → verification
```

with reversal tests, controls, uncertainty and a closed system boundary.
