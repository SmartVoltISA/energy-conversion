# Foundation

## Question

What is energy?

The repository does not begin by inventing a new definition. It begins by separating established physics from hypotheses.

## Working distinctions

**Energy** is a conserved physical quantity associated with the state of a system and its ability to undergo transformations and transfer energy.

**Transfer** is an exchange of energy between systems or subsystems.

**Conversion** is a change in the form in which energy is represented within the chosen system boundary.

**Work** is energy transfer associated with an organized interaction that can be described mechanically or through generalized forces.

**Heat** is energy transferred because of a temperature difference.

**Power** is the rate of energy transfer.

## State

A useful abstract representation is:

`S(t) = {matter, fields, motion, configuration, constraints}`

Energy is a function of the physical state:

`E = E(S)`

A process changes the state:

`S1 → interaction → S2`

and therefore changes the distribution of energy among degrees of freedom or transfers energy across the system boundary.

## Conservation

For a closed system, total energy is conserved. For an open subsystem, energy accounting must include all relevant inflows, outflows, storage changes and exchanges.

The bookkeeping must always specify the system boundary.

## Engineering question

The practical research target is not "create energy". It is:

> Find physical transitions that move a larger fraction of available energy into a desired output with acceptable cost, complexity, rate, material limits and controllability.

## Critical warning

A lower number of boxes in a block diagram does not prove higher efficiency. Direct conversion may remove intermediate stages while introducing new losses or physical constraints.

Therefore every proposed shortcut must be tested against an explicit energy balance.

## Status vocabulary

- `KNOWN` — supported by established theory or reliable measurement.
- `CALCULATED` — obtained from a stated model and inputs.
- `OBSERVED` — measured in an experiment.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — proposed explanation requiring testing.
- `UNKNOWN` — not established.
- `REJECTED` — contradicted under the stated conditions.
